What do these numbers mean?

To review: puzzles you have tried before that are due to come back today.

New: puzzles you have not tried yet.

Learned: puzzles you have solved first try three times in a row. They only come back every 7 days, then every 21 days.

Solve a puzzle first try, without a hint, and it comes back after a longer gap each time (1, 3, 7, then 21 days). Get it wrong and it starts again. The numbers count only the puzzles that match the filters above.
